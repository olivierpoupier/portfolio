My name's Olivier.

I have a big interest in visual arts, industrial design and overall functionnal stuff. From the very start, I was drawn by the idea that we can engineer solutions to make our daily lives better.

I'm a full-stack developer and systems architect with one simple belief: Solutions should solve specific challenges elegantly rather than pretending to solve many superficially.

Coding has been part of my life since I was 9. In high school I started participating in different robotics competitions, first doing Lego robots but rapidly switching to [FIRST robotics](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/frc/) I participated as a student for 3 years and then mentored for the start of a season until Covid hit. At 16, during a competition I helped a PhD student from University of Michigan fix a disconnected wire and a few falling bolts on a robot he was demoing. He then mentored me in the building of a robot arm over the next few months, during which I learned how to use CAD software and the basics of PCB design.

Also during my high school years, at 15, I started participating in hackathons. This allowed me to work on many different projects with diverse teams from all sorts of backgrounds. It allowed me to explore a very broad range of stacks and tools to build anything from: A news articles agregation systems, A mobile app to help refugees have access to redcross services to a recipe website to help plan meals for the week. Through one of the hackathons I met a local tech CEO sponsoring the event who allowed me to visit his company's office for a one day look-around which turned into my first job as a developer.

After high school I started making websites for artists and brands which led me to meet WallyPDF who allowed me to work with A$AP Rocky's [Awge](https://awge.com). Through this experience I ended up working on many websites but also on brand marketing plans and acquiring more real world experience by starting a few little brands with different partners.

I have also been making an explicit effort to work in a broad variety of fields and contexts. This allowed me to deepen my skills and to apply my knowlege to systems of varying scales; from systems handling terabytes of data everyday to simple apps for a few users. 

Looking ahead, I’m keen to deepen my work in robotics-adjacent systems, embedded electronics, and cybersecurity. The intersection of hardware, software, and real world impact really excites me — and I’m ready to tackle projects that push the boundaries of my skills.

My Approach & Values
	•	User-centric thinking: I believe every technical decision should relate back to the person using the system.
	•	Elegant over flashy: It’s easy to add complexity, harder to simplify. I aim for the clean solution.
	•	Curiosity-driven: If I’m not exploring a new tech, reading a new paper, or tinkering with a board, I’m not satisfied.
	•	Collaborative mindset: Even as a freelancer, I see myself part of a team: with the client, with users, with stakeholders. Communication matters.
	•	Security-aware: In a world of growing threats, I don’t just build — I build with awareness of what could go wrong, and how to make it hard to go wrong.
